## What CoreX Capital AI is

CoreX Capital AI publicly positions itself as a **financial-technology infrastructure provider and AI company**. Its public ecosystem combines real-time financial infrastructure, agentic intelligence, market-analysis products, custom automation, brokerage technology and an independent control/governance philosophy.

The white-label documentation explicitly separates CoreX from the brokerage operator: CoreX builds and maintains technology infrastructure; it states that it does not hold brokerage client funds or act as the market counterparty.

## CoreX Signal AI

The public CoreX Signal AI product presents an explainable AI command center with surfaces for:

- cross-timeframe coherence;
- execution and risk context;
- probabilistic / Monte Carlo forecasting;
- institutional depth mapping;
- attribution and impact drivers;
- market-regime context;
- macro/NLP intelligence;
- factor-cluster analysis.

Its published architecture describes a path from **MT5 Broker Data → Python Quant Engine → Flask-SocketIO → WebSocket → PWA client** and lists HMAC-SHA256 authentication, dual-pipeline delivery, data-integrity concepts and global edge delivery. The product site labels dashboard previews and performance-style examples as simulated/illustrative where applicable.

## AxisOption

AxisOption is a separate client-facing options platform in the broader ecosystem. Its public materials describe TradingView chart-based trading, one-click directional interaction, multi-asset markets, demo/live environments, configurable expiry choices, direct client support and an industry-standard white-label technology relationship with CoreX Capital AI.

AxisOption publishes its own Terms of Service and Risk Disclosure. Trading and financial markets involve risk of loss and geographic restrictions can apply.

## Strategy OS™

Strategy OS™ is CoreX's published custom-engineering path for traders, funds, asset managers, brokers and B2B platforms. The public process is described as:

1. define strategy / objective / constraints;
2. design the dedicated AI and control architecture;
3. train and simulate;
4. apply control and veto layers;
5. deliver the client-owned system.

## Governance philosophy

A recurring CoreX principle is that **AI confidence is not final authorization**. Public material separates probabilistic intelligence from independent control, stability and risk constraints. Physics Core and the Autonomous Veto Protocol express that governance philosophy at a public conceptual level.

Strong implementation claims and proprietary mechanisms on the official product pages are presented in this repository as **published CoreX descriptions**, not as independently benchmarked or audited results.
